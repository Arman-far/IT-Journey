# Network types classify networks based on their **size**, **coverage area**, and **purpose**. Each type is designed to connect devices in different environments, from a small home network to the global Internet.
---
1. PAN (Personal-Area-Network) : Connects personal devices over a very short range, such as Bluetooth between a phone and headphones.
(Like everybody , desktops , airpods,phons )
2. SOHO (Small office-Home office ) : A network designed for small businesses or home offices, typically combining wired and wireless devices with a single router. 
(so small one)
3. LAN (Local-Area-Network) : Connects devices within a small area, such as a home, office, or school.
 (bigger that soho)
4. WAN (Wide-Area-Network) : Connects multiple LANs over long distances, such as between cities or countries. The Internet is the largest WAN.
 (can we say LAN but a service provider is in this network)
5. MAN (Metropolitan-Area-Network) : Covers a city or metropolitan area by connecting multiple LANs.
 (for example 2 hospital wants to connect to each other but they dont want a service provider , they connect with theit own ways)
6. CAN (Campus-Area-Network): Connects multiple LANs across a campus, university, or business complex.
 (like in a University area , you can dig and let pass the wires in there or add wires across the buildings)
7. SAN (Storage-Area-Network) : A dedicated high-speed network used to connect servers to storage devices.
( Like it named , for storages )
8. WLAN (Wireless-Local-Area-Network) : A wireless version of a LAN that uses Wi-Fi instead of cables.
